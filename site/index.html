Why Camza

Studio power.
One app.

Every tool a pro camera needs — real-time, on Linux, with zero fuss.

Pro HD Magic Enhancer

One tap applies a pro sharpness kernel, contrast bump and virtual studio ring light for crisp, high-end footage.

Anti-Blowout Shield

Directly controls the V4L2 sensor exposure & gain to instantly fix blown-out white backgrounds. No more overexposure.

Live Video Filters

Real-time WebGL shaders — Cyberpunk, Vintage, Thermal, Matrix and more — with live intensity control while you record.

Burst Mode

Rapid-fire 5 photos in quick succession with a single click, with smart auto-save — never miss the perfect moment.

Smart Auto-Saving

Photos and videos are automatically organized into ~/Pictures/Camza and ~/Videos/Camza.

Premium Dark Glass UI

Floating glass controls, micro-animations, live filter previews and full hardware sliders — beautiful and fast.
